## W4.12 product review finding

The persisted RC01 v8 pilot initially exposed an hourly/bundle commercial frame for Dirección Jurídica Externa (`Bolsa mensual`, `Horas`, `Atención dentro de la bolsa`). That framing conflicts with the v8 commercial model: the recurring service is presented publicly through coverage, matter complexity, priority, service level, governance and escalation rather than a public bundle of hours.

## Resolution architecture

The historical v4.2 service catalog remains unchanged because it is shared by the certified v7.4 legacy surfaces. W4.12 therefore does not rewrite legacy truth.

Instead, v8 now has an explicit, fail-closed presentation layer for RC01:

1. legacy source remains canonical and byte-preserved for historical surfaces;
2. `experience-model-v80.json` declares exact `path / from / to` presentation replacements;
3. `render_v8_pilot.py` verifies each historical `from` value before applying a replacement;
4. only a constrained set of commercial-framing fields may be overridden;
5. residual public `bolsa` / `hora(s)` framing causes renderer failure;
6. materialization consumes the governed effective v8 presentation source;
7. browser regression coverage rejects recurrence of hourly public framing.

This preserves source traceability while allowing v8 to improve commercial presentation without mutating v7.4.

## RC01 commercial frame after correction

The v8 surface now communicates:

- `Cobertura mensual definida`;
- typologies of matters, complexity levels, priority, SLA and escalation rules;
- attention within agreed coverage and service levels;
- estimated demand rather than public hourly volume;
- temporary coverage expansion as an explicit supplement.

The service continues to state that availability is not unlimited and that extraordinary projects remain separately scoped.

## Public-tree persistence hardening

During certified refresh, the Public Tree workflow exposed a separate persistence condition: refresh required all three v8 targets to be staged even when only RC01 had changed.

The workflow was corrected while keeping atomicity fail-closed:

- bootstrap still requires exact `3/3` targets;
- refresh accepts a non-empty subset only from the exact three-route allowlist;
- unexpected staged paths fail the job;
- `git diff --cached --check` remains mandatory;
- persistence remains downstream of generated Browser/link/Axe PASS;
- production branches are not written by this workflow.
